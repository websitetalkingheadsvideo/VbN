# 🩸 Kerry, the Desert-Wandering Gangrel
*“The mountains remember what the city forgets.”*

---

## 🎭 Storyteller Appendix (Laws of the Night: Revised)

### ROLE IN THE CHRONICLE
Kerry is a bridge character — the last living witness to the 1973 Prince’s staking, and the only one who knows where the body lies.  
He’s not an enemy; he’s a *living mystery* and a *moral test*. PCs who approach him with compassion may unearth the chronicle’s central secret — those who exploit or harm him will bury it forever.

---

## ⚖️ ROLEPLAY NOTES
- Kerry’s demeanor oscillates between lucid and feral.  
- In bright moonlight or open spaces, he’s calm and articulate.  
- Underground or confined, his **claustrophobia** intensifies — he becomes erratic, gasping, muttering, and trembling.  
- The “voices” he hears (actually his deranged mind) sometimes echo truths; use them to foreshadow key events.

---

## 🧭 TRUST PROGRESSION SYSTEM

| Stage | Trigger / Player Action | Kerry’s Response | Notes |
|--------|-------------------------|------------------|--------|
| **0 – Wary** | PCs meet Kerry, neutral or aggressive tone | Cautious, evasive, avoids eye contact | **Social Challenge (Diff 2)** using *Empathetic*, *Honest*, or *Compassionate* traits |
| **1 – Intrigued** | PCs offer food, vitae, or genuine conversation | Mutters about “the one beneath the mountain” | Will not yet confirm it’s the Prince |
| **2 – Confidant** | PCs return for a second night or show kindness | “I can take you there. Two nights’ walk…” | Will guide them if they agree |
| **3 – Devoted** | PCs protect him from harm or frenzy | Calls PC “moon brother/sister” | Becomes Ally; offers wilderness insight |

> **Failure Conditions:** Mockery or Dominate use → **Frenzy Challenge (Diff 6)**. Betrayal → he flees into the desert.

---

## 🌒 STAGING THE DESERT JOURNEY

### First Night
- Kerry appears near Elysium or an abandoned yard.
- If treated kindly, he hints: *“I seen where they buried the heart that beats without a pulse.”*

### Second Night
- Agrees to lead PCs “to the secret.” Two-night trek through desert and canyons.  
- Use mirages, whispers, and animal signs to build tension.

### Final Approach
- Freezes at cave mouth (**Claustrophobia**).  
- PCs must **soothe** him with a **Social Challenge (Diff 4)** or he frenzies and flees.  
- If calmed: *“Don’t… look too long when you see him. He looks back.”*

---

## 💀 FRENZY TRIGGERS

| Situation | Challenge | Result |
|------------|------------|---------|
| Enclosed or underground space | Self-Control (Diff 6) | Flight or bestial panic |
| Betrayal or physical threat | Courage (Diff 5) | Full frenzy |
| Near the Prince’s body | Self-Control (Diff 7) | Psychic pressure → attack or flee |

**Abilities:** Brawl x2, Survival x3  
**Disciplines:** Protean 3 (Feral Claws, Earth Meld), Fortitude 2, Animalism 2  
**Retests:** Survival or Fortitude  
**Damage:** Feral Claws (Lethal, +1 damage trait)  
**Fortitude:** 2 free retests vs. damage  
**Behavior:** Fights only to escape or protect his secret.

---

## 🧩 STORYTELLER NOTES
- Portray him as *half prophet, half animal*; play instability with empathy.  
- His fear of the cave should infect the players — make silence terrifying.  
- If PCs win his trust, he’s a recurring guide.  
- If they lose it, the desert swallows both him and the truth.
